Call (stated by gift, resolving the pathless leftover): a transfer line whose counterpart naming
lands on the very book it sits in is **not** answered by any transfer — the ledger refuses a
transfer that goes to itself, and the refusal is correct. The human counts the money as what it
truly was: an expense it paid, or income it received. The old transfer line is voided and the
amount re-enters in the same book, same day, with a category picked from the cash book.

The mechanism follows the shape already ratified by the reconciliation work: the classifier only
names the reading (self-transfer), the card never pretends its bulk confirm can answer it, and the
row itself carries the two countings behind the same guards every lone-line write has (paired,
dismissed, draft, voided, non-transfer — each refused with the words that fix it).

Cost: a counted line is not reversible by reopening — undoing it is the counter-batch reverse path,
shared with every other adjustment. Accepted: the void keeps the words on the record, and counting
is the one human answer no rule could guess.
