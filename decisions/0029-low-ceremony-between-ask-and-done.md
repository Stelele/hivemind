# 0029. Low ceremony between ask and done

**Status:** accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-06
**Affects:** every project

## Context
Bare `proceed` / `you can proceed` appears 150+ times as the standard plan-approval turn across cms-system and erpnext-dashboard sessions.

## Decision
Present a plan, get approval, execute. Do not narrate steps or ask permission for obvious actions.

## Consequences
A short path from request to working code. The cost is less visibility between plan and result for people who want narration.
